About

Physiotherapy built around how you move

Physiotherapy helps people recover from injury, manage pain and rebuild function through assessment, therapeutic exercise and clear guidance. Myorebuild Physiotherapy is an outpatient clinic in Kharadi, Pune, serving residents and professionals nearby.

Whether you are managing everyday aches or returning to activity, the aim is simple: understand your concern, then plan a sensible route forward together.
